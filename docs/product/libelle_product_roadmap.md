# Libelle Product Roadmap

Author: Vinay Venkateswaran

#### Requirement - Platform to connect volunteers (V) to Non-profits (N).

#### P0 scope

The Non-profit is TCUS. The objective is limited until a volunteer contract is signed with the non-profit.

#### Constraints:

Low cost, virtually $0 investment of development, testing, deployment

#### Painpoints for the non-profit:

1. Discovery:
    1. It is difficult to get visibility of your non-profit, the work that they do, in order to attract volunteers.
    2. Already listed on a platform, but it is hard to collate and update so many entries at different platforms.
2. Listing (Role and responsibility description):
    1. It is difficult to create a listing and manage its lifecycle (update status of the listing as and when you find people) (especially with multiple platforms for discovery)
    2. It is difficult to estimate how many volunteers are apt for a listing.
    3. It is difficult to balance how strict vs lapse the listing requirements should be.
3. Selection: 
    1. Of the volunteer options available, there is difficulty in selecting a subset.
        1. Selecting a subset involves going through multiple candidates’ applications.
        2. It might be difficult to know whether the subset is motivated to volunteer for the Non-profit.
    2. Of the subset selected, there is difficulty in mapping specific volunteer to specific role.
        1. There can be a disparity between intent and ability of a volunteer.
        2. Some activities might have pre-requisites to be done. If a volunteer is ready to do task B, but task A is dependent on task A, then the volunteer cannot help until a volunteer for task A is found, or, the volunteer is capable and willing to do task A as well.
    3. Candidate application:
        1. Resume (Capability)
        2. Why do you want to join non-profit? (Intent)
        3. What would you like to do for the non-profit? (Intent)
        4. Relevant experience with what you would like to do? (Capability)
        5. How much time are you willing to spend? (Intent)

#### Questions:

1. How is discovery managed for a Non-profit (TCUS)?
2. How is listing managed for a non-profit (TCUS)?
3. What is the selection process of a non-profit. Does it vary between a non-profit that has:
    1. an abundance of volunteers reaching out?
    2. limited number of volunteer reach out?
    3. Sparse reach out intervals
4. Why would a non-profit not want to select everyone who applies?
5. What is the definition of “Successful volunteer found” for TCUS? Would it be different for another non-profit? Is it safe to assume that there are several non-profits that would have selection criteria similar to that of TCUS?
